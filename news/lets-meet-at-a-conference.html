Let's meet at a conference!

2/22/2019

I (Gabriel) am coming to/presenting at a number of upcoming conferences, and I'd love meet people interested in joining the lab. In the near future-
  • Biophysical Society (Mar 2-4, Baltimore) - Talk Sunday Mar 3 11:15am in the "Proteins: Exploring Sequence Space via Computation and Experiment" Symposium
  • American Chemical Society (Mar 31-April 3, Orlando) - Poster Tues April 2 6pm-8pm in the Biomedical Technologies poster session, West Hall C
  • Protein Engineering Summit (PEGS) Boston (April 10-12, Boston) - Talk Thurs April 11 9:05am in the "Emerging Technologies in Antibody Engineering" session
  • Hydrogen-Deuterium Exchange Mass Spectrometry (HDXMS) 2019 (May 21-24, Banff)
  • Proteins Gordon Research Conference (June 16-21, Holderness)

Hope to see you there, e-mail me if you're interested in talking!
